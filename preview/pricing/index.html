Why publish it at all

A price you can see is the first proof that the work behind it is real.

We kept a genuine entry point at ₹16,000 a month on purpose, because a small business that is not yet ready for a full engagement still deserves work done properly, not a watered-down version sold as a favour. And we kept real depth above it, because a business ready to treat growth as a core function should never have to wonder whether its agency is out of its depth.

Wherever you start, the promise is the same: the figure you see is the figure we build against, every result is reported against the leads and enquiries it generated rather than vanity metrics, and nothing about the arrangement locks you in. That is what transparent pricing is for, not a discount, but a signal that there is nothing here we need to hide until you are already on a call.
