How we measure a result

Four numbers we report, and can prove.

Leads and enquiries

The forms, messages and enquiries the work brings in, attributed to the channel that earned them.

Calls and bookings

Booked calls and appointments, counted through the call and booking tracking we set up, not guessed at.

Cost per lead

What each lead actually costs to earn, so spend is judged on what it returns, not on how busy it looks.

Rankings and organic traffic

Where you rank for the terms that matter, and how much qualified organic traffic that visibility brings, month on month.
