# Meta ad copy — /growth/ campaign

Copy for the two creatives running into one ad set: the 15-second video and
the static (4:5, 9:16, 1.91:1).

Framework, per `docs/content-frameworks.md`: **Pixar beats, tighter** — the
same structure the scripts use. Routine → disruption → consequence →
resolution, compressed into a feed post.

## Two rules this copy is written to

**The first 125 characters are the whole ad.** Meta truncates the rest behind
"See more" on mobile, and most people never open it. Every primary text below
carries its argument in the opening line and uses the rest to earn the click,
not to introduce the idea.

**The copy must not repeat the creative.** The video and the static already
say *your business doesn't need more marketing*. Primary text that says it
again wastes the one place you get to add something — so the copy picks up
where the creative stops and answers the question it provokes: *fine, so how
would I know which part is broken?*

# Video ad (15s)

## Primary text — A

> Most businesses don't have a marketing problem. They have a diagnosis problem.
>
> SEO, ads, content, social, a website — all of it running, none of it running
> together. The gap between them is where the growth quietly goes.
>
> Adding more of any one channel doesn't close that gap. Knowing which one is
> leaking does.
>
> Tell us what's not working. We'll look at the whole picture and tell you what
> actually deserves attention first — even if that turns out to be something we
> don't sell.
>
> No generic pitch. No obligation to hire us.

**Headline:** You Don't Need More Marketing
**Description:** Tell us what's not working.

## Primary text — B (shorter, for the test)

> You're not short of marketing. You're short of an answer.
>
> Traffic with no enquiries. Ads you can't read. Content nobody finds. Each one
> looks like its own problem, and usually they're one problem wearing five
> costumes.
>
> Send us what isn't working and we'll tell you where the gap actually is.

**Headline:** Which Part Isn't Working?
**Description:** One enquiry. A straight answer.

# Static ad (4:5, 9:16, 1.91:1)

Deliberately a different angle from the video, so the two don't read as the
same ad twice to anyone who sees both.

## Primary text — A

> Five channels running. Nothing connecting. That's not a budget problem.
>
> Most agencies answer "we're not growing" by selling you another service. It's
> the easiest sale in the industry and it's usually the wrong one, because more
> of a channel that isn't converting just costs more.
>
> We start with the problem instead of the service — search, conversion,
> content, website, paid, AI search — and tell you which one is actually
> holding the rest back.
>
> Every price we charge is published on the site before you ever speak to us.
>
> Tell us what's not working.

**Headline:** Doing More Isn't Growing More
**Description:** Find the gap first.

## Primary text — B (the disqualifier)

> We're probably not the right agency for you.
>
> Not if you want guaranteed rankings, cheap monthly packages, followers for the
> sake of numbers, or an agency that says yes to everything you ask for.
>
> But if you want someone to look at the entire picture and tell you what
> actually deserves attention — including the parts we'd make no money fixing —
> that's the whole job.
>
> Tell us what's not working.

**Headline:** We May Not Be Right For You
**Description:** Straight answers only.

## Notes

**Subtitles: don't add any.** The video is kinetic typography — the words are
already on screen, sized for a phone. Burned-in captions would print the same
sentence twice in the same frame. Leave Meta's auto-captions off for this
creative too; they'll overlay the lower third where the CTA sits.

**Spelling.** Every form on the site says **Enquiry**; the creative's button
currently says `INQUIRY`. The copy above uses the site's spelling. Worth
settling one way and making the button match, since a viewer sees both within
seconds of each other.

**No statistics anywhere in this copy, on purpose.** Nothing here needs a
number to work, and every number in this campaign would have to be one of the
real, sourced client figures — which carries a client-approval requirement the
copy doesn't otherwise need. See `docs/ads/meta-video-ads-growth.md`.

**Claims to keep true.** "Every price published" is checkable on `/pricing/`.
"Even if that turns out to be something we don't sell" is a promise the sales
conversation has to actually honour — it is the strongest line in the set and
the one most easily made a lie.
