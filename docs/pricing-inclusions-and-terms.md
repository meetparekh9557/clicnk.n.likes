## Part A — Competitor benchmark (what the market does)

**Takeaway: your pricing sits at the accessible end of the global market and is
competitive-to-premium in India — which fits the "serve both, anchor high"
goal. Your real edge is not price; it's showing exactly what's included when
most competitors hide it behind a call.**

- **SEO retainers** globally run **$500–$25,000+/mo**, with the legitimate band
  at **$1,500–$10,000** and a median near **$3,000**. Entry packages ($1,000–
  $3,000) cover one or two channels; mid boutiques ($3,000–$8,000) add
  dedicated account management. Standard inclusions: keyword research, on-page,
  technical, content, link building, monthly reporting.
- **Social management** runs **$500–$5,000/mo** typically; agency tiers go
  Basic ($2k–$6k) → Growth ($7k–$12.5k) → Strategic ($13k–$20k) → Enterprise
  ($25k+). Basic ≈ 12–16 posts, 2 platforms, community mgmt, monthly report +
  one strategy call.
- **Website builds in India**: starter **₹20k–₹60k**, growth **₹75k–₹2L**,
  scale **₹2L–₹6L+**. A 5–15 page CMS site is typically ₹25k–₹1.5L. Higher
  tiers add integrations, performance, maintenance; entry tiers = core build,
  limited revisions.
- **PPC/Google Ads**: two separate costs — **management fee** (10–20% of spend,
  or a flat $500–$5,000/mo) and **ad spend** (paid to Google). Your model of a
  flat management fee with ad spend billed to the client is standard best
  practice.

## Part C — "What's included / not included" (client-facing clauses)

Show these near the plan builder so a prospect knows exactly what they're
paying for:

**Every engagement includes:** a documented strategy, a named point of contact,
all execution listed for your tier, reporting tied to real leads and enquiries,
and the free live audit that sets the honest starting point.

**Always billed to you directly, never routed through us:**
- **Ad spend** (Meta / Google) — paid from your own card on the platform.
- **Domain, hosting, and any CMS or plugin licences** — bought in your name.
- **Paid stock, fonts, or premium third-party tools** — only if you approve them.
- **Photo / video shoot production** — arranged via our shoot partner, billed
  separately.

**How it works:** prices are indicative starting points; your exact scope and
figure are fixed in a **written proposal** before any work begins. Monthly
services can be paused or cancelled with 30 days' notice. Nothing locks you in.
